FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is OISIS? +

OISIS is a modern digital experience designed to help people organize ideas, projects and workflows in one beautiful environment.

How do I create an account? +

Click the Get Started button and complete the signup form with your name, email and password.

I forgot my password. What should I do? +

Go to the login page and use the password recovery option to reset your account password.

Is my information secure? +

OISIS is designed with privacy and account security in mind. Always use a strong, unique password for your account.

How can I contact the OISIS team? +

You can contact us anytime through the Contact Support button above.
